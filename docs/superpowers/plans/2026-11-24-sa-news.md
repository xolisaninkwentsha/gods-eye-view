# SA News Layer Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add a fork-local layer, "SA news (24h)" (`sa-news`). It shows South African GDELT stories placed at city level, plus headlines from SA outlets placed by matching town names. One dot per story; its card opens the article.

**Architecture:**
- A same-origin, keyless server provider (`server/providers/saNews/`) serves `/api/sa-news`. It reads a 24-hour window of GDELT 15-minute event exports, filtered to South Africa, and polls 8 outlet RSS feeds. It matches feed headlines against a committed list of towns, merges the two by URL, and keeps everything in memory.
- A client layer (`src/layers/saNews/`) draws screen-space dots in a sunflower spiral per place. Its overlay card links out.

**Tech Stack:** plain ES modules, `node:test` and the app's draw port. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-sa-news-design.md`. Read it first: it holds the measured facts (197 GDELT stories a day; headline yield 7.6%), the rules and the anti-goals.

### Task 1: GDELT SA parser (pure)

**Files:** create `server/providers/saNews/gdelt.js` and `server/providers/saNews/gdelt.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces:**
- `SA_COL`: `{id: 0, rootCode: 28, quadClass: 29, mentions: 31, geoType: 51, place: 52, country: 53, lat: 56, lon: 57, added: 59, url: 60}` (GDELT 2.0, 61 columns).
- `parseSaEvents(table: string, {denied}) → Event[]`, where `Event` is `{id, root, quad, mentions, place, lat, lon, addedMs, url}`. Keep a row only when:
  - it has at least 61 fields;
  - the country is `SF`;
  - `geoType` is `3` or `4`;
  - lat/lon are finite and not 0,0;
  - `articleLink(url)` is http(s);
  - `denied(place)` is false (the `place`'s first comma part).

  `addedMs` comes from DATEADDED (`YYYYMMDDHHMMSS`, UTC).
- `saKind(root) → 'protest' | 'violence' | 'event'`: root `14` is protest; `18`, `19`, `20` are violence; anything else is event.
- `saStories(events, {nowMs, windowMs = 86_400_000}) → Story[]`:
  - group by `storyKey`;
  - place via `choosePlaces` (first chosen place);
  - the story's kind is the most severe of its events (violence over protest over event);
  - `publishedAt` is the earliest `addedMs`, and stories older than the window are dropped;
  - title is `cleanTitle(addressWords(url).join(' '))` when there are 3 or more words, else `Story from <domain>`;
  - `source` is the domain without `www.`;
  - `id` is `gd:` plus a short stable hash of the normalised URL.
- `normalizeUrl(url) → string | null`: https, host lowercased with `www.` removed, no query or hash, no trailing slash. Returns null for anything that is not http(s).

  - kinds come from roots 14, 18 and 04;
  - grouping by URL gives one story;
  - a story older than 24 h is dropped;
  - title from address words, with the "Story from" fallback;
  - `normalizeUrl` cases, including `javascript:` giving null.

### Task 2: GDELT window fetcher

**Files:** extend `server/providers/saNews/gdelt.js`; create `server/providers/saNews/gdeltWindow.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces:** `createGdeltWindow({fetchImpl, now, log, denied})` returns `{refresh({signal}) → Promise<void>, stories() → Story[], coverage() → {hoursCovered, missing, state: 'loading'|'ok'|'partial'|'failed'}}`.
- The export list is `exportsOfDay(now(), 24)`, cut at the newest export named by `fetchLatestExport` (never ask past it).
- Each call fetches up to 12 missing exports, 4 at a time:
  - `fetchImpl(exportUrl)` with `redirect: 'error'`, the User-Agent, and a 30 s timeout combined with the caller's signal;
  - the body is read with `readResponseBytesCapped` at 8 MiB, then `unzipExport(bytes, 8 MiB)`, then `parseSaEvents`.
- A 404 counts as missing. After 3 tries, or 90 minutes missing, the export is marked gone.
- Parsed events are kept per export; exports that fall out of the window are pruned.
- `hoursCovered` is the number of consecutive quarter-hours present from the newest, divided by 4.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** (stubbed fetch and clock):
  - it never requests past `lastupdate`;
  - it fetches at most 12 per call and at most 4 at once;
  - a 404 is retried, and after 3 tries it is gone;
  - the window prunes old exports;
  - `coverage().hoursCovered` grows across calls until 24;
  - a fetch error leaves the held stories intact, and the state reflects it;
  - the User-Agent and `redirect` are asserted.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run the tests; they fail.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run the tests; they pass.

**Interfaces** in `places.js`:

  - Joburg and Jozi → Johannesburg;

### Task 4: RSS reader

**Files:** create `server/providers/saNews/rss.js` and `server/providers/saNews/rss.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces:**
- `SA_FEEDS`: an array of `{id, name, url}` for the 8 feeds in the Global Constraints. Names: IOL, Daily Maverick, TimesLIVE, Mail & Guardian, GroundUp, eNCA, The Citizen, SAnews.
- `parseFeed(xml, {outlet, nowMs}) → Item[]`, where `Item` is `{title, url, publishedAt}`:
  - reads RSS `<item>` and Atom `<entry>`;
  - decodes CDATA and named and numeric entities;
  - links must be http(s);
  - `publishedAt` comes from `pubDate`, `updated` or `published`, falling back to `nowMs`;
  - drops items older than 24 h, or more than 1 h in the future;

- `createFeeds({fetchImpl, now, log})` returns `{refresh({signal}), items() → Array<Item & {source}>, status() → {[id]: 'ok'|'failed'|'loading'}}`.
  - Each feed is refreshed every 15 minutes, 4 at a time.
  - Reads use `readResponseTextCapped` at 2 MiB, a 20 s timeout, the User-Agent and `redirect: 'error'`.
  - A failed feed keeps its previous items and is marked failed.

### Task 5: Provider `/api/sa-news`

**Files:**
- Create `server/providers/saNews/index.js` and `server/providers/saNews/provider.test.mjs`.
- Modify `server/providers/local.js`: mount after `saGovernanceProxy()`.
- Modify `src/tooling/previewServing.test.mjs`: plugin `sa-news`, and `['/api/sa-news/unknown', 404]`.

**Interfaces:** `saNewsProxy({fetchImpl, now, log} = {})` returns the plugin `sa-news`, mounted at `/api/sa-news`. A `GET /` returns:

```
200 {fetchedAt, stories, status: {gdelt: {hoursCovered, missing, state}, rss: {[feedId]: state}}}
```

- **Stories:**
  - GDELT stories, plus feed items whose `matchPlace(title)` is not null (`kind: 'headline'`, `id: 'rss:' + hash`, `place` from the match).
  - Merged by `normalizeUrl`: when both sources have the same URL, keep one story with the outlet's title, GDELT's place and coordinates, GDELT's kind, and both sources noted.
  - Sorted newest first.
- **Refresh:**
  - The first request awaits the first GDELT and feed refresh, capped at 10 s, then answers with what it holds.
  - After that, a background loop continues:
    - GDELT every 2 s while `missing > 0` and the window is not complete, else every 5 minutes;
    - feeds every 15 minutes.
  - Refreshes are coalesced.
  - The route never waits on a refresh once something is held.
- **Gates:** same-site gate, rate limit (`makeRateLimiter({windowMs: 60_000, max: 60, globalMax: 600, now})`), 404, 405 and 429.
- **Logs:** code or name only. Use the `errorLabel` pattern from `server/providers/saQuakes.js`.

  - the combined payload has both kinds;
  - one story per URL across both sources;
  - unmatched headlines are excluded;
  - a slow upstream still gets an answer within the cap;
  - the status shows partial coverage and a failed feed;
  - 404, 405 and cross-site 403;
  - concurrent requests share one refresh.
